## Recording notes

- Use a **code or first name only**; a full name is not needed
- If the subject does not want to be identifiable, film hands, back of head or audio only — **state this in the notes column**
- If a subject withdraws consent afterwards, **remove the material from all public material immediately**

## Record the interview setup, so the edit is not misread later

Fill this in for each subject. **This is not bureaucracy:** if someone later reviews the clip without knowing the setup, a Mandarin question followed by a Cantonese answer is easy to misinterpret, and a Macau interviewee's account is easy to mistake for a Hong Kong one.

## What the consent covers

By agreeing, the subject allows:
- the clip to be shown during the HacKU 2026 exhibition and pitching round
- the clip to be published in the public repository `github.com/yc-eagle/med-safe`
- the clip to be included in the 3-minute demo video

**They do not have to agree to all three.** Record precisely what was agreed, and edit accordingly.
