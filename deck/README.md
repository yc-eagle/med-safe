## Timing discipline

**8 teams in 80 minutes means roughly 6-7 minutes each including Q&A.** Write to 6 minutes and keep margin.

If you have to cut, cut slides **12 and 14** first. **Never cut slides 5, 8, 9, 11 or 13** — those five carry the most marks.

## The one slide that must be prepared: competition

**This is the only criterion that can be won purely through preparation.**

| Existing option | What it does | What it does not |
|---|---|---|
| General-purpose AI assistants | Anything you ask | Do not know local drug names; need a connection; **answer confidently and wrongly** |
| The leaflet in the box | Official and accurate | Cannot be read by the people who need it |
| Drug lookup apps and pharmacopoeias | You can find the drug | **Give information, not judgement**; require a connection |
| Community pharmacist services | Real professional judgement | **Cannot reach "right now, at home"** |
| **Us** | **Give a judgement at that moment and mark where it is unsure** | — |
