# Data attribution

Hong Kong SAR Government, Department of Health, Drug Office supplies the original registered-product catalogue and schema (snapshot 2026-09-25). [Catalogue](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-dh-dh_do-hk-dh-do-pharmaceutical-product) · [DATA.GOV.HK terms](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions). Retain attribution, source dates and notices; government endorsement is not implied.

Structured rules and short factual summaries cite Hong Kong Drug Office, DailyMed label records and MedlinePlus/ASHP item-by-item. Source links, document dates, sections and SHA-256 records remain, while full downloaded clinical HTML and US label XML are excluded from the public repository. Source rights are not transferred to MedSafe.

Complete software/model/data notices are in [`../CREDITS.md`](../CREDITS.md). All 14 rules remain pending professional review. The catalogue's registration fields do not establish personal medication suitability.
